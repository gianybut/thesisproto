Dashed boxes are the model's drafts. Whatever is on screen when you save is stored as your label.

Rules

  • The picture is the near zone only. Label everything in it.
  • D00 longitudinal: crack along the direction of travel. D10 transverse: across the lane. One box per continuous crack; new box if it bends more than ~45° or has a gap over ~1 m.
  • D20 alligator: interconnected cracks forming closed blocks. Box the whole patch; cracks touching it belong to it.
  • D40 pothole: box the edge of the hole, not the cracks around it.
  • Don't label sealed or patched cracks, joints, road markings, shadows, manholes, wet streaks.
  • Unsure? Press S rather than guess.
